# London Marathon 2027 Training Plan

**Race:** Sunday 25 April 2027, London. **Goal:** 2:45:00 (6:17/mi). **Start:** Monday 5 October 2026. **29 weeks.**

Read `INTERVIEW.md` for the why. This file is the what. `plan.json` is the same plan for the dashboard.

## Coach's honest note, said once

Current fitness (4:26 marathon on 9 weeks, 2:14 half, sub-25 5K) predicts a marathon between 3:50 and 4:30 today. 2:45 needs a sub-18:00 5K and a sub-1:20 half. No published case of that jump in 29 weeks exists. The plan below is the most aggressive build the calf history and a 5-day week allow. The time trials are the referee. If they say no, the dashboard will say no, and the decision is yours.

## Rules

1. Weekly miles never jump more than 10% above the highest week so far. Down week every 4th week, about 25% off. Built into the table.
2. Long run never more than 40% of the week. Built in.
3. Thu club 5K: easy ceiling except weeks 4, 12, 19, 26. If the group drags you, drop off the back. Dashboard flags a non-TT Thursday over HR 160.
4. Calf raises Wed and Sun after the run: 3 x 15 straight-knee, 3 x 15 bent-knee, single leg, slow. Add weight when 15 is easy. Dashboard has a checkbox.
5. Fuel every long run over 90 min from week 1: gel every 40 min, water with electrolytes, first gel at 30 min. Weeks 18, 21, 24: full race rehearsal, race breakfast, race gels, race timing.
6. Red Whoop recovery: the dashboard coach may swap a hard day for easy. It will not cancel the long run unless recovery is red **and** sleep is under 6 h **and** HRV is below your 30-day baseline.
7. Calf twinge: stop the run. Tell the dashboard. Three easy days, then restart. Never run through it.
8. Two missed runs in a week: do not make them up. Carry on.

## Phases

- **Base, wks 1–8 (5 Oct–29 Nov):** 21 → 31 mi. All easy. Build the habit, the calf and the gut. Track with the slow group.
- **Build 1, wks 9–15 (30 Nov–17 Jan):** 34 → 46 mi. cMP segments enter the long run. Gate 1.
- **Build 2, wks 16–24 (18 Jan–21 Mar):** 48 → 58 mi. Wed tempo. Three 20+ long runs. Gate 2. Peak week 24.
- **Taper, wks 25–28 (22 Mar–18 Apr):** 58 → 30 mi. Keep cMP touches, drop volume.
- **Race week, wk 29.**
